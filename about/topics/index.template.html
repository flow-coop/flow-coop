About Flow: Topics

Topic pages provide a flexible infrastructure to describe data flows of value to co-op members and how they are connected.

Topics can be subscribed to, and you can join the discussion using an ActivityPub client, e.g. Mastodon.

A good topic page describes a broad enough concept that benefits from discussion and elaboration over time. It should be associated with more than one possible data flow and relationships with other topics should be explored before creating it.

New topics can be proposed. They are created if they are aligned with the co-op's mission and a commitment is made by two coop members to active moderation and curation. Pages with fewer than two active curators are marked as under consideration for mothballing. On a regular basis, pages are reviewed and marked as inactive with content marked as read-only and interactions limited.
