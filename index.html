Flow coop

This is the WIP home of the Flow coop, a cooperative building hosting and services where your data flows work for you.

It currently hosts discussion pages for data flows.

Note: This site is still experimental.

The Flow Coop provides a high quality service you would feel comfortable recommending to family and friends. Members work in the open. They experiment to prove value, and plan sustainable investments in services with operating expenses covered on a user-pays opt-in basis. Services are implemented and maintained with high reliability, stability, and user friendliness, putting user trust first.
